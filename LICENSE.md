Testrium uses a mixed, versioned licensing model.

trading as Zarpyon, are offered under the PolyForm Small Business License
1.0.0. Uses outside those terms require a separate commercial agreement with
the licensor.

Releases through version 0.2.0 remain available under the MIT License.
Contributor-owned portions originally received under MIT remain under MIT
unless their copyright holders separately agree to different terms in writing.

Applicable documents:

- `LICENSE-POLYFORM.md`: community-use terms for Zarpyon-owned portions.
- `LICENSE-MIT.md`: retained MIT terms for historical releases and applicable
  contributor-owned portions.
- `NOTICE.md`: version boundary, scope, and commercial-licensing details.
- `AUTHORS.md`: authors and project participants.

Where terms conflict for a particular portion, the license granted by that
portion's copyright holder controls. Nothing in this repository revokes rights
already granted for earlier releases.
